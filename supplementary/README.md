retain their accepted scientific contents and structure. Visible author lists
name Xu Jin, Bihang Cui, Ruxin Yan, Yingchen Mao and Dinglin Zhang, with Ruxin Yan

## ESM_3b sheet-specific conditions

- `External_Datasets`, `External_Samples`, `Mabin_Accessions` and
  `Citation_Verification`: the author's new source checks and curation use
  CC BY 4.0. Retain cited source records and the RNAdecayCafe attribution where
  applicable. This does not grant exclusive rights over identifiers or facts.
- `Saluki_Human_Sources`: includes material from Saluki article Table 1.
  Article/table expression follows the article's CC BY 4.0 statement; article
  data follow its CC0 statement unless credited otherwise. Preserve attribution
  and exceptions; do not extend this CC0 statement to all Saluki repository files.
- `Saluki_Script_Crosswalk`: `assignment` contains original Saluki R code
  excerpts. These retain the upstream Apache-2.0 conditions and
  [license notice](../docs/licenses/SALUKI_REPOSITORY_LICENSE.txt). The author's
  new crosswalk organization is CC BY 4.0; the code excerpts are not relicensed.

The same distinctions apply to corresponding source CSVs and
`data/sources/supplementary_display.xlsx`. Author-owned Figures 1–4 and S1–S2
visualization contributions are licensed under CC BY 4.0 within the author-owned
scope. Source data and third-party figure components retain their applicable
terms and remain outside that author grant.

See [DATA_LICENSES.md](../DATA_LICENSES.md) for author/source attribution,
license links and exclusions, and [docs/file_manifest.csv](../docs/file_manifest.csv)
for exact file provenance, rights scopes and checksums. No blanket license is
asserted over every cell of a mixed workbook or over the complete ESM_1 prose.

Standalone Figure S1/S2 PNG, PDF and SVG files are in `../figures/`. The PNGs
are 600 dpi exports, not the smaller screen previews. For submission, use ESM_1
and the three workbooks above; `source/` provides the editable supplementary
document. Statistical CSV/NPZ files remain in the reproduction repository and
are not duplicated as many small reader-facing tables here.
